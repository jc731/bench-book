persona to write each marker before acting, as its instructions say.

## Findings

### F1. Floor and bench look the same

- Screen: live game, both halves.
- Event: 1, and every sub after it.
- Expected: after Starters, a way to see who is on the floor and who is on the bench.
- Did: looked at the cards after setup and after each sub.
- Happened: the persona saw no divider, shading or tag. #22 and #33 "look exactly like the
  starters"; only the hidden labels say "bench". The controller's end-of-game screenshot
  agrees: #12 and #22 (bench) are hard to tell from the five above them. This is 0.10's
  deliberate neutral-card design ("cards after the top five are one shade grayer"), and the
  shade did not register with a first-timer. The persona did every sub correctly anyway,
  and the end lineup is right.
- Severity: hesitated.
- Suggested direction: owner's call. This sits under the open "Starters and the lineup:
  revisit" backlog item; the run is one data point that the one-shade cue is not seen.
- Screenshot: v0.11.0/end-floor-bench.png

### F2. A substitution can't be put in an earlier quarter

- Screen: Check Q2, Book, live game.
- Event: 32, 33.
- Expected: after adding #33's missed Q2 foul, a way to say #33 went in for #10 in Q2.
- Did: looked for subs in the check's play list and in "Add play", which offer only scoring
  and fouls. At the start of Q3, used Sub to put #33 in for #10.
- Happened: the floor ended right. But the persona could not tell whether the sub belongs
  to Q2 or Q3 ("the sub is recorded as Q3, not Q2, so I'm not sure it is recorded
  correctly"). Since 0.11 the late foul no longer puts #33 on the floor now, so the "6 on
  in Q3 with no word" half of v0.9.0's F1 is gone; the past-quarter sub itself still has
  no home.
- Severity: hesitated.
- Suggested direction: owner's call, as in the backlog: the check's Add play could ask who
  went out, or say plainly that subs aren't kept per quarter so a current sub is fine.
- Screenshot: v0.11.0/q3-33-still-bench.png (Q3, #33 still in the bench slot after the Q2
  foul was added)
- **Scenario note for the owner:** `## Expected end state` says event 32's foul "puts #33
  on the floor by itself (six on)" and the app then hints #10 "longest without a play".
  Under 0.11's rule ("A play added or re-credited after its quarter never moves anyone onto
  the floor") neither happens, so the scenario text is stale. The end lineup it expects is
  still right. Not changed here: the audit edits only its report.

  On the live screen each half says "Home" and "Away", not "Test Home" and "Test Opp";

  given, would cost no taps.

### F5. A bench player's basket is taken with no warning

- Screen: live game.
- Event: 20.

  The persona caught it from the confirmation line and Undo restored everything. This is

- Severity: polish. Theoretical: no unnoticed slip happened. Trigger: a real game where a
  basket lands on a bench player and nobody notices until the quarter check.
- Suggested direction: owner's call; likely no change.
