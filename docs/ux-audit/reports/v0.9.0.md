# UX audit v0.9.0

## Summary

- Version 0.9.0, 2026-11-17, persona: bookkeeper, app URL `http://localhost:4410/`.
- `pnpm ux:stats` exited 0: no disallowed tools, one navigation (the app URL), and the
  received prompt matched the built prompt file. 171 actions in all.
- Events: 49 done, 4 hesitated, 0 failed, 0 skipped. No outcome was adjusted on action
  counts (see the note under Events). Wrong entries found by the export comparison: **0**.
  Both CSVs agree with the expected end state on every score, player line, team foul,
  timeout and check, for both teams.
- The lineup at the end matches the expected one: Test Home #3, #5, #7, #10, #33 on the
  floor (#12 and #22 on the bench); Test Opp #2, #11, #14, #24, #45 (#21 out).
- The persona ended the game and saved both files itself. The controller read the files
  the persona downloaded, then tapped "Back to game" on the Final screen to read the
  lineup. No entry was changed.
- Top 3 problems:
  1. There is no way to record a substitution in an earlier quarter (event 33). The persona
     entered it as a current sub and could not tell which quarter it belongs to.
  2. Adding an unlisted opposing player is only discoverable after picking a play, and the
     opponent side has no Sub button while five are on (event 35).
  3. In the "Who's on the floor?" picker, a tapped player stays under her old heading. Only
     the shading and the count change, so a swap doesn't look done until Done (event 5).
- These are leads from a single run, not a scorecard.

## Findings

### F1. A substitution can't be put in an earlier quarter

- Screen: Check Q2 / Log / live game.
- Event: 32, 33.
- Expected: after adding #33's missed Q2 foul, a way to say she went in for #10 in Q2.
- Did: looked in the Log, the check screen and the ⋯ menu, which list plays and timeouts
  but no subs. Then, in Q3, tapped the amber "Sub · 6 on" and picked #10, marked "likely".
- Happened: the floor ended right, and the expected lineup is met. But the persona couldn't
  tell whether the sub was stored in Q2 or Q3. And adding the Q2 foul had already put #33
  on the floor in Q3 with no word about it, so the screen read "6 on" mid-third-quarter.
- Severity: hesitated.
- Suggested direction: owner's call. Either let a past-quarter play's floor change be
  resolved where it's made (the check's "Add play" could ask "who went out?" for a bench
  player), or say in the feedback bar that the add also put her on the floor now.
- Screenshot: v0.9.0/past-foul-six-on.png

### F2. A new opposing player is found only after picking a play

- Screen: live game, opponent half.
- Event: 35.
- Expected: a way to check in an unlisted player (#45) for #24, then record her three.
- Did: found no Sub button on the opponent side (it appears only when the count is off
  five). Tapped the opponent's "+ Play", chose +3, got "Whose number?", typed 45. Then
  used the amber "Sub · 6 on" to take #24 out.
- Happened: totals and lineup are right, but the basket was entered before the check-in,
  so the plays file's order differs from the game.
- Severity: hesitated.
- Suggested direction: make adding a player, or subbing, findable on the opponent side
  before a play is chosen.

### F3. Sub picker: players don't move between "On the floor" and "On the bench"

- Screen: "Who's on the floor?"
- Event: 5.
- Expected: tapping #7 and #22 to visibly swap them.
- Did: tapped both.
- Happened: #22 stayed under "On the bench" and #7 under "On the floor". Only the shading
  and the "N on the floor" count changed, so the persona wasn't sure until Done showed
  "#22 (Home) in for #7".
- Severity: hesitated.
- Suggested direction: move a tapped player under the other heading, or make the selected
  state read as "on the floor" without a second look.
- Screenshot: v0.9.0/sub-picker-headings.png

### F4. "Full: 3" doesn't say whether it counts left or used

- Screen: timeouts, both halves.
- Event: 18.
- Happened: the persona briefly doubted, and seeing it drop from 3 to 2 settled it.
- Severity: polish.
- Suggested direction: owner's call; a word such as "left" would remove the doubt.

### F5. Ending Q1 to Q3 has no confirmation; "End Q4" is red

- Screen: live game, end-of-quarter button.
- Event: 13, 30, 43, 52.
- Happened: Q1 to Q3 end with one tap. The persona was fine with that but noted that a
  slip would end a quarter. "End Q4" is red, which read as danger and made the persona
  pause. The label is no longer cut off (v0.6.0 F1 is gone).
- Severity: polish. Theoretical: no stray tap happened in this run. Trigger: a run or real
  game where a quarter is ended by mistake. The red is deliberate: the README's color table
  gives red to "ends something for good", End Q4 included. The pause is that color doing
  its job.
- Suggested direction: owner's call; likely no change.

  persona confirmed it on the Starters screen.
- Severity: polish.
- Suggested direction: owner's call on the POS column. A short line about starters near
  the roster.

    single-shot FT panel (F7), two Saves (F8), hidden "Check" meaning (F10).
